Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Order Cancellation

  • Before Dispatch: You may cancel your order at any time before it is dispatched from our warehouse. The full amount will be refunded to your original payment method.
  • After Dispatch: Once an order is handed over to our delivery partners, it cannot be cancelled. You may refuse delivery, and once the RTO (Return to Origin) is complete, a refund will be issued minus a standard deduction for forward and return shipping.

2. Returns Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery, subject to the following conditions:

  • The product must be unused, uninstalled, and in its original packaging with all tags, accessories, and manuals intact.
  • Products damaged during installation by a plumber/technician are strictly non-returnable.

Mandatory Unboxing Video

To claim a replacement or refund for items damaged in transit or missing parts, a clear, continuous unboxing video from the moment the sealed package is opened is mandatory. Without this video, claims for physical damage or missing items will not be entertained.

3. Refund Processing

  • Once your returned item is received and inspected at our facility, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Approved refunds are processed strictly to the original payment method (via Stripe).
  • Please allow 5 to 7 business days for the credited amount to reflect in your bank account or credit card statement.

4. Contact Us

For any return or cancellation requests, please contact our support team:
