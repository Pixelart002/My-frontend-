LUVIIO

Luviio is an India-focused hardware and sanitary products brand and ecommerce platform.

Luviio focuses on practical products for everyday home, kitchen, bathroom and drainage needs, with product discovery, useful information and direct online shopping.

Website architecture:
- Public brand, SEO and product discovery: https://luviio.in
- Ecommerce application: https://app.luviio.in
- Blog and guides: https://luviio.in/blog

The public website is intended for brand information, product discovery, categories, guides and SEO-friendly product pages. The ecommerce application is intended for shopping, cart, checkout, account and related transactional workflows.

Luviio aims to make hardware and sanitary product shopping simple, clear and useful for customers in India.
